## Interpretation

Under fresh imaginary prompts, five endpoints had description-form rates of 100% and zero binary description disagreement, but nonzero four-state disagreement. Their four-state disagreement was entirely U versus Marked E. This distinguishes cue matching from whether a description is produced; it is not evidence of truthfulness or factual reliability.

A reports separate generation-noise and word-bootstrap scales. It does not yield an optimal sample size, a variance-explained percentage, or proof that original trials were unnecessary. Its depth curves and full-corpus zero nested deviation partly follow directly from the formulas. All results remain post hoc surface-measure analyses.
